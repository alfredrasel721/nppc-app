PARENTAL CONTROL PLATFORM - FEATURES & FUNCTIONS CHECKLIST
Based on the earlier review of 18 unique apps (19 submitted Play Store links).
Updated: 2026-11-06

HOW TO USE
[ ] = feature to consider / track during product planning
[MVP] = suggested core feature; [ADV] = advanced / optional feature
This is a consolidated feature shortlist, NOT a claim that every reviewed app
provides every item. Advanced items may be OS-, permission-, plan- or region-dependent.
Some operational/privacy items are recommended design features, not verified
competitor features.

10. PRIVACY, SAFETY & PLATFORM REQUIREMENTS
============================================================
[ ] [MVP] Age-appropriate notice, consent and transparency
[ ] [MVP] Clear indication of sensitive monitoring; no covert surveillance
[ ] [MVP] Data minimization, encryption and retention/deletion controls
[ ] [MVP] Guardian identity verification and secure account recovery
[ ] [MVP] Emergency access and fail-safe behavior when a device is locked
[ ] [MVP] Test Android/iOS differences before promising a feature
[ ] [MVP] Explain permissions and handle permission revocation gracefully
[ ] [ADV] Accessibility, localization and low-bandwidth support
[ ] [ADV] Periodic access reviews and privacy/security audit reports

NOTES
- [MVP]/[ADV] are product-planning recommendations, not competitor ratings.
- The previous reports distinguish documented features from inferred and
  proposed functions. Consult them for app-by-app evidence and limitations.
- Monitoring of calls, messages, microphone, camera and screen can be highly
  sensitive and is restricted or unavailable on many platforms.
- Do not treat this checklist as proof that a particular competitor supports
  a feature on a particular operating system or subscription tier.
