# Device Compatibility Test Matrix

## Minimum test set

At least Android 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 and current stable as available; Pixel and two major OEMs; phones and tablets; background battery optimization on/off.

## For each capability

Record Android API, OEM, device owner status, permissions, Google Play policy status, foreground/background behavior, user experience, test date and evidence.

## Blockers

Do not advertise universal screen mirroring, notification filtering, social content filtering, app-install approval or non-removable app controls without device evidence.
