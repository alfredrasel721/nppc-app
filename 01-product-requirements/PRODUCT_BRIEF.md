# Product Brief

## Problem

Families need transparent, manageable Android digital-safety tools without paywalling ordinary controls and emergency features.

## Users

Primary parent, additional guardian, child, trusted emergency contact, restricted support operator; optional school and compatible wearable roles.

## Value

Separate, easy-to-understand parent and child apps; comprehensive free core; only six sensitive monitoring/filtering capabilities require Premium and additional consent.

## Success

Pairing success, rule sync correctness, request round-trip, SOS delivery, child understanding of monitoring, zero unauthorized live sessions, billing entitlement integrity.
