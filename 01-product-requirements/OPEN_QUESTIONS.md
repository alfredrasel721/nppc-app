# Human Decisions Needed

## Product

Brand, application IDs, target ages, launch country/countries, languages, guardian identity/consent rules.

## Android

Minimum supported SDK, whether to support fully managed devices, permitted enforcement methods and Play policy approval.

## Billing

Subscription prices, duration, SKU, refunds/grace policy, Play Console configuration.

## Integrations

Maps provider, push service project, auth provider, live media signaling provider, optional school/wearable vendors.

## Legal

Privacy policy/controller identity, retention periods, child rights and regional consent rules.
