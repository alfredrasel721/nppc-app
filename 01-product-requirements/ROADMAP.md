# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — feasibility

Verify Play policies, Android device management, child protection requirements, permissions, pricing and regional laws.

## Phase 1 — free foundation

Two apps, auth, family, pairing, roles, app/time limits, child dashboard, requests, sync, emergency, basic location, privacy controls.

## Phase 2 — free breadth

All remaining checklist Free features where feasible, including tasks, chat, reports, exports, geofences, advanced alerts and optional integrations.

## Phase 3 — Premium

Billing, web/social/notification filters, then separately reviewed child-approved live screen/camera/mic. Each gated behind human approval and OS/policy proof.

## Phase 4 — release

Security, accessibility, Play declarations, internal testing, closed testing, incident runbooks and human sign-off.
