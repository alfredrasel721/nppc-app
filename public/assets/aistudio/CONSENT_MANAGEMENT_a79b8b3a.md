# Consent and Transparency Management

## Onboarding

Verify authorized guardian, present child-friendly supervision notice, request each Android permission at point of use and explain what is collected.

## Premium live sessions

A Premium purchase does not authorize capture. Each session must show child a specific guardian, media mode and duration, allow explicit acceptance/decline, require OS permission and show foreground indicator/stop.

## Filter disclosures

Show what notification/web/social filters can and cannot see; no claim to read encrypted chats.

## Revocation

Child can stop live capture; Android permission revocation stops functionality; guardian can unpair; all events audited with no media payload.
