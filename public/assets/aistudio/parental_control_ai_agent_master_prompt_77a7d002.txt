NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT RULES
- Build TWO SEPARATE installable Android applications: one Parent App and one Child App, with distinct app IDs, launchers, role experiences, and release artifacts. Shared internal modules are allowed.
- Android only. Do not build iOS apps or a family-facing web dashboard. An access-restricted internal administrative console may be created if needed for operations.
- All source-checklist features are FREE except exactly these six PREMIUM groups: (1) screen mirroring/live screen viewing; (2) live camera; (3) live audio; (4) notification filtering; (5) web filtering; and (6) social-media app/content filtering.
- FREE explicitly includes family accounts, pairing, guardians, activity reports and history, GPS/location and geofences, SOS, screen-time limits, schedules, app blocking and app-use limits, requests and approvals, child wellbeing, chat, chores/rewards, emergency contacts, data rights, and other checklist functions, subject to technical feasibility. The checklist's ADV label describes development complexity, NOT billing tier.
- Preserve the distinction: ordinary application blocking is FREE, but social-media content/category filtering is PREMIUM; browsing/search history reporting when possible is FREE, but active web filtering is PREMIUM.
- Never paywall emergency access, child-facing explanations, permission management, consent, safety alerts, account security, privacy controls, or data deletion.
- Enforce Premium entitlements server-side and in the authorized child-device behavior; design for billing cancellation, renewal, refund, grace periods, offline periods, and entitlement resynchronization.

SAFETY, PRIVACY, AND ANDROID CONSTRAINTS
- No covert surveillance, stealth installation, silent remote microphone/camera activation, invisible screen capture, permission bypass, certificate-based TLS interception, bypass of encrypted private-message protections, or abusive anti-uninstall mechanisms.
- Every live screen, camera, and microphone session must require an explicit action or approval on the Child App, all applicable Android OS permissions and consent prompts, a visible foreground indicator, and a child-accessible stop control. Session duration and activity must be auditable.
- Handle notifications, Accessibility, device administration, usage access, location, app blocking, and other sensitive permissions only through documented, policy-compliant Android capabilities. Do not claim capabilities unsupported by the OS or distribution channel.
- Preserve emergency calling, SOS, recovery access, and critical OS notifications. Use least-privilege data access, authenticated family/guardian roles, encryption, retention limits, explicit disclosures, and abuse-prevention procedures.
- Validate current Android and Google Play policy before implementing or shipping restricted functions. When policies or platform behavior prevent a requested capability, provide a safe alternative and log the gap.
- Require human approval for legal/age policies, sensitive-feature launch, platform declarations, billing production configuration, credentials, and public release.

EXECUTION PLAN
PHASE 0 — DISCOVERY AND VALIDATION
Inventory all files and feature IDs. Inspect repository readiness, dependencies, API drafts, schema drafts, and source stubs. Check Android platform feasibility and current Play policy. Produce a gap report, risks, dependencies, and a prioritized executable task plan. Flag unresolved critical decisions without stopping safe foundational work.

PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION
Establish the multi-module repository, two Android applications, approved technical stack, backend, database/migrations, environment templates, authentication foundations, CI checks, secure configuration, and local development instructions. Create separate build targets and clear role/permission boundaries.

PHASE 2 — FAMILY AND DEVICE ONBOARDING
Implement parent registration/login, child enrollment/pairing, family profiles, invitations and guardian roles, child-friendly disclosures, permission onboarding, device status/sync, unpairing and recovery. Validate unauthorized pairing and role-access denial.

PHASE 3 — FREE CORE CONTROLS
Implement screen-time dashboards, app usage reporting, device/app limits, school/bedtime schedules, ordinary app blocking, essential-app exceptions, extra-time requests, parent approvals, offline enforcement where supported, and reliable rule synchronization. Build real Android behavior rather than only UI simulations.

PHASE 4 — FREE SAFETY AND COMMUNICATION
Implement location with timestamps, geofence events, SOS and fallback contacts, battery/offline alerts, child check-ins, guardian collaboration, family chat, history and reports, tasks and rewards, and account/data-rights screens. Add optional integrations only after core functions and necessary validations.

PHASE 5 — BILLING AND PREMIUM ACCESS
Integrate the subscription system and independently test all six Premium entitlement boundaries. Premium must never unlock collection before the required permissions and child approval are valid. Existing Free capabilities must continue after Premium expires.

PHASE 6 — SENSITIVE PREMIUM FUNCTIONS
Implement each Premium feature behind feature flags and safety reviews, one at a time: screen-view sessions, camera sessions, audio sessions, notification filtering, website filtering, and social-media content/category filtering only as OS-supported. Document foreground sessions, consent, retention, emergency fallbacks, unsupported devices, test coverage, and precise user-facing capability descriptions. Do not substitute covert monitoring for unavailable APIs.

PHASE 7 — ADMINISTRATION, RESILIENCE, AND QUALITY
Complete restricted support/admin operations as needed; test disconnections, permission revocations, OS process kills, offline sync, billing edge cases, child device changes, denial of consent, data deletion, and guardian revocation. Perform accessibility, performance, security, and abuse-case testing. Verify on emulator AND physical Android devices where required.

PHASE 8 — RELEASE PREPARATION
Prepare two independent signed-release workflows using secrets managed outside source control, store listings, privacy disclosures, policy declarations, test artifacts, deployment documentation, backup/recovery and monitoring. Do not publish, spend money, or use live production keys without explicit human approval.
