# Incident Response

## Severity

SEV1 child safety breach or unauthorized capture; SEV2 account takeover/location exposure; SEV3 availability degradation.

## Workflow

Detect → contain sessions/tokens → preserve minimal evidence → notify security/legal → remediate → assess required notifications → postmortem.

## Runbook

Kill-switch for all Premium live sessions and filters; rotate compromised keys; revoke affected devices; verify logs contain no raw media.

## Human authority

Security lead and legal counsel approve user/regulator communications.
