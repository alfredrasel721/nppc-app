# Design System

## Principles

Trustworthy, non-covert, age-appropriate, calm. Parent and Child have distinct branding accents and application icons but share accessible components.

## Tokens

8dp spacing grid; touch targets ≥48dp; dynamic type; semantic colors success/warning/error; minimum contrast 4.5:1 for body text. `design-tokens.json` is the single source.

## Components

Child selector, device health, sync status, limit slider, schedule editor, permission explainer, request/approval card, premium entitlement badge, live-session consent sheet, persistent session stop button.

## States

Loading, empty, offline, permission denied, unsupported OS, subscription expired, sync pending, child declined, session ended.
