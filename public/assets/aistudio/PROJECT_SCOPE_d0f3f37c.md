# Project Scope

## In scope

All checklist items as candidate requirements, classified Free except the six user-named Premium families; Android parent and child; backend; internal admin; optional integrations as separate phases.

## Not in scope

iOS, customer web app, hidden surveillance, unsupported privileged controls, reading private app databases, bypassing E2EE.

## Conditional scope

Wearables, school, call/SMS data, browser history, content classification and driving reports require a feasibility and policy spike; do not promise until validated.
