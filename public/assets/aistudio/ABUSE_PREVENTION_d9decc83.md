# Abuse Prevention

## Controls

Rate-limit guardian invites and live requests; enforce linked-child scope; alert on new device/guardian; session audit visible to child and family owner; allow incident reporting.

## Prohibited behavior

Covert mic/camera, stealth screenshots, hiding indicators, bypassing OS prompts, stalking non-family devices, exfiltrating notification content.

## Escalation

Security team reviews credible abuse with minimal data; documented account suspension and appeal; emergency contacts do not gain routine tracking.
