# Internal Admin and Support

## Scope

Restricted internal console may be browser-based, but not a consumer-facing family web product.

## Permissions

Tiered support roles, SSO/MFA, audit logs, least privilege, just-in-time access; no child camera/audio/screen content access.

## Functions

Pairing/sync diagnostics, support tickets, abuse reports, entitlement reconciliation, incident status, account/data deletion, retention and regional settings.

## Deployment

Private network or zero-trust access; production admin actions require approval and logging.
