# Free vs Premium Entitlements

## Exactly six Premium families

1. Screen mirroring / live screen view (child-approved MediaProjection session).
2. Live camera (child-approved foreground camera session).
3. Live audio (child-approved foreground microphone session).
4. Notification filtering (only app/category-level suppression/allow where Android permits; never hide critical OS/emergency notifications).
5. Web filtering (domains/categories/allowlist/blocklist; no TLS interception).
6. Social-media app/content filtering (app access controls may remain free; content/category filters are Premium; no promise of reading encrypted in-app messages).

## All other features

FREE, including features previously labeled [ADV]. This includes app blocking and per-app screen time limits; these are not the same as Premium social content filtering. Ordinary browser/search history reporting remains Free if lawful and supported, but web content blocking/filtering is Premium.

## Guardrails

Entitlement check on backend and child enforcement; deny Premium operations when expired/refunded; preserve Free controls and SOS; never gate privacy or consent UI; billing is Android Parent app only.

## Plans

Plan price, trial, SKU, region and tax policy NOT specified by user; require human configuration before launch.

## Special cases

Optional wearables, school integrations and third-party service fees are not app Premium features; integrations can be unsupported or external-cost dependent but must not be silently reclassified Premium.
