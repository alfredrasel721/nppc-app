# Retention Policy — Proposed Defaults Pending Legal Review

## Suggested defaults

Usage aggregates 90 days; detailed locations 30 days; SOS events 90 days; pairing codes 10 minutes; live session metadata 30 days; rule history 90 days; support logs 30 days; billing audit per statutory obligations.

## No media storage

No screen, camera or microphone media recordings by default. No notification body storage by default.

## Controls

Per-data-category TTL jobs, export/delete workflows, backup expiry, access review. Human counsel must approve final durations and regional exceptions.
