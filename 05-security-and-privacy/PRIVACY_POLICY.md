# Privacy Policy — Draft for Counsel

## Notice

This is a drafting outline, **not** a legally approved privacy policy. Human counsel must fill controller identity, address, jurisdiction, age thresholds, subprocessors and contact.

## Data categories

Parent account, child profile, device diagnostics, usage aggregates, location if enabled, SOS, requests, family chat, billing entitlement; optional sensitive live session metadata.

## Purpose

Provide guardian controls, child-visible status, family communication and safety alerts; not advertising or resale.

## Rights

Parent access/export/deletion subject to applicable child privacy laws; age-appropriate child notices and protections.

## Sensitive features

Premium screen/camera/audio require active child approval and OS permissions; default no media recording; notification bodies not collected by default; no TLS interception.

## Retention

See `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`; final durations and lawful basis require human approval.
